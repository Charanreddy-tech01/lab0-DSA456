
Binary Search Tree and AVL Balancing Report
-------------------------------------------

1. Overview
-----------
In this assignment, we generated 1000 Binary Search Trees (BSTs) from random permutations of numbers 1 through 20. 
Each BST was analyzed for its height and root imbalance (difference in height between left and right subtrees).

2. Height and Imbalance Analysis
-------------------------------
- Minimum Height: 5
- Maximum Height: 12
- Average Height: ~7.77

- Minimum Imbalance: 0
- Maximum Imbalance: 11
- Average Imbalance: ~3.61

Histograms were created to visualize the distribution of tree heights and imbalances across the 1000 BSTs.

3. AVL Tree Rebalancing
-----------------------
Two trees with the highest imbalance values (11 and 10 respectively) were selected.
The AVL Tree algorithm was applied using rotations to restore balance.

- Tree 1:
  - Original Height: 12
  - Imbalance: 11
  - Balanced Height: 5

- Tree 2:
  - Original Height: 11
  - Imbalance: 10
  - Balanced Height: 5

These results confirm that AVL self-balancing significantly improves the structure and efficiency of BSTs.

4. Conclusion
-------------
This experiment shows how unbalanced trees frequently occur in BSTs generated from random data, and how AVL rotations
can restore balance efficiently. Balanced trees enable faster search, insert, and delete operations, especially important in large datasets.
